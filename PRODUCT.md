# Travel product context

## Register

product — the roadbook templates are practical travel tools. The repository gallery is a separate brand surface described in site/DESIGN.md.

## Users and purpose

Travelers and their coding agents turn supplied plans and materials into usable, shareable roadbooks. They may travel alone, with friends/family, for business or across borders. Existing examples are starting points; new tasks may require new templates and modules.

## Existing design decisions

Preserve the distinct trail, rail, island and business themes. The gallery uses the user's selected moss-green paper/Zine style. Do not spread one trip's motifs or rules across every template.

## Usability priorities

Readable facts, reachable controls, clear unknowns, stable state, mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard access and reduced motion. Containers and decorative surfaces must not clip or squeeze their text; overlay failures must not trap the user.

These facts are drawn from the existing repository and the user's accepted requirements. This file adds no new product scope or approval gate.
